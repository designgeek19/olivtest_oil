About Us

Olivtest Planet Energy Limited is your dependable partner in petroleum product supply and energy support services across Nigeria. As a key player in the Olivtest Group, we are committed to delivering high-quality diesel, engine oil, lubricants, and other petroleum products to both businesses and individuals. Whether you need large-scale deliveries for commercial operations or smaller volumes for personal or institutional use, we provide flexible, reliable solutions tailored to your energy needs.

Beyond fuel distribution, we offer a comprehensive range of on-site energy services designed to keep your operations running smoothly. These include professional tank cleaning, diesel generator servicing, parts replacement, and precise calibration services to ensure your systems operate efficiently and safely. Our technicians are trained to respond swiftly and deliver technical support with professionalism and attention to detail.

As we continue to grow, our vision remains focused on sustainable energy solutions that drive performance and peace of mind. At Olivtest Planet Energy Limited, we’re not just fuel suppliers—we’re your energy partners, working with you to keep your engines running, your equipment calibrated, and your operations powered with excellence.
